## Summary

- **ERP & GL (8 public + many private)**: Mature oligopoly (SAP, Oracle, Workday, Microsoft) + mid/SMB (Intuit, Xero, Sage)
- **AP / Spend (2 public + 5 large private)**: Rapidly evolving, many private
- **Close / Disclosure (2 public)**: BlackLine + Workiva, stable
- **FP&A (2 public + many private)**: Anaplan/Coupa private-takeouts, OneStream IPO'd 2024
- **Tax (5 public)**: Large caps (Intuit, TRI, WTKWY) + specialized (Vertex)
- **Billing (Adobe + Salesforce)**: Major players hold billing modules
- **Modern Payments (15+ public)**: Wide variety by vertical + model
- **Legacy Payments Infrastructure (11 public)**: Established large caps
- **Commercial Banking (9 public)**: Treasury + transaction banking adjacencies
- **Vertical Fintech (10+ public)**: Emerging markets + consumer lending
- **Insurance (5 public)**: Benefits + risk brokers
- **Consulting (6+ public)**: Finance-adjacent services

**Public universe: ~90 tickers across finance & accounting tech.**
