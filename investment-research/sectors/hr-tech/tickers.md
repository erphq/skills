- **HCM / Payroll (12 public + many private)**: Stable leaders, mid-market most competitive

- **Staffing (13 public)**: Labor-market cyclical; bellwether for economic cycles
- **Learning & Dev (10 public + private)**: Consumer + enterprise split; AI disruption reshaping
- **Benefits Brokers (7 public)**: Consolidating oligopoly, reliable compounders
- **Retirement / Benefits Admin (9 public)**: Mature insurance/financial plays
