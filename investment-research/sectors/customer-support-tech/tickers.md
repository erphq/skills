## Take-Private Case Studies (Useful Reference)

- **Zendesk** (2022) — Permira + Hellman & Friedman at ~$10B, ~6× revenue
- **Qualtrics** (2023) — Silver Lake + CPPIB at ~$12.5B, ~7× revenue
- **Medallia** (2021) — Thoma Bravo at $6.4B
- **UserTesting** (2022) — Thoma Bravo at ~$1.3B
- **Verint** (2024) — Thoma Bravo at ~$2B
- **Genesys** — Private for >10 years, ~$21B valuation in 2021

These case studies inform what strategic + PE buyers pay for CX tech. Multiples provide comps for current public names.
