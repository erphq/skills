# Training & Development — 100 to 1,000 People

## What This Process Does

Learning & Development at this scale is **a dedicated function with structured programs + real budget.** A L&D manager or director (reporting to CHRO) leads 1–5 specialists. Programs include role-based learning paths (engineering technical ladders, sales certification, product PM curriculum), leadership-development cohorts (first-time-manager program, senior-leader development), compliance training (annual required trainings at scale), skills marketplace (internal mobility, gig matching), conference + certification budgets, and executive coaching for senior roles.

Budget typically $500–$2,000 per employee per year plus program-specific spend (leadership development $20K–$50K per cohort member, executive coaching $15K–$40K per engagement). The goal: **develop capability, drive retention, enable internal mobility, and position the company as a place where great people grow.** Well-run L&D reduces regrettable attrition 10–20% + strengthens recruiting narrative.

## Build — Setting It Up

### With Agents

- **Learning-path personalization**: Agent curates learning based on role + level + development goals + skills gaps. Updates continuously as skills evolve.
- **LMS content orchestration**: Agent manages content library — curated external (LinkedIn Learning, Coursera, Pluralsight), internal (recorded talks, codified knowledge), vendor-specific (sales tooling, product deep-dives). Assigns relevant content per role.
- **Compliance-training automation**: Agent tracks required trainings per role + state + industry — anti-harassment (varies by state), security awareness (SOC 2, PCI, HIPAA where applicable), DEI, regulatory (FinServ: SEC/FINRA continuing education). Schedules, assigns, tracks completion, escalates non-completion.
- **Leadership-program management**: Cohort-based programs (first-time-manager, senior-leader). Agent manages applications, selections, curriculum delivery, coaching coordination, alumni network.
- **Mentor matching at scale**: Agent pairs mentors/mentees based on development areas, function, seniority, availability. Facilitates touchpoint cadence + captures relationship health.
- **Skills-marketplace operation**: Internal job postings, gig opportunities, project-based skill development. Agent matches employees to opportunities based on skills + interests + career goals.
- **Conference + certification budgets**: Request workflows, approval routing, post-event knowledge sharing (internal talks from conference attendees).
- **Learning-analytics dashboards**: Engagement rates, completion rates, skills-growth tracking, NPS on programs, correlation to retention + performance.

### Key Decisions

1. **LMS platform**: Docebo (flexible, AI-driven), 360Learning (collaborative), Absorb (enterprise), Workday Learning (if on Workday HCM), LinkedIn Learning (content + light platform). Integration with HRIS is critical.
2. **Learning-stipend amount**: $500 minimum at mid-market; $1,000–$2,500 competitive; $2,500+ for senior/IC+ levels. Split between self-directed + company-curated often.
3. **Leadership-development strategy**: Build-vs-buy — in-house curriculum (tailored, organizational fit, slower to scale) vs external programs (CCL, Stanford LEAD, BetterUp, Bravely — proven quality, less organization-specific).
4. **First-time-manager program**: Critical intervention. Cohort-based, 3–6 month program, includes curriculum + coaching + peer-learning + mentorship. $5K–$15K per participant.
5. **Executive coaching**: For VP+ roles, senior IC+ roles. External coaches (BetterUp, Bravely, Torch, Hogan assessments). $15K–$40K per engagement. ROI measurable through retention + performance.
6. **Compliance training sources**: NAVEX, Ethena, Traliant for anti-harassment; KnowBe4, Hoxhunt for security awareness; specialty vendors for industry-specific (healthcare HIPAA, finance BSA/AML).
7. **Skills-marketplace approach**: Gloat, Eightfold, Fuel50 for dedicated platforms; LinkedIn internal mobility; custom-built if on Workday. Skills marketplace requires skills taxonomy first.
8. **DEI learning investment**: Beyond compliance anti-bias training — DEI leadership development, employee resource group (ERG) programming, community of practice facilitation.
9. **Conference policy**: Per-level budget, relevance criteria, post-event share-back expectation. Equitable access across protected categories.

### Common Mistakes

- **LMS as compliance-only tool**: If LMS only hosts required compliance content, engagement near zero. Must host developmental + useful content too.
- **First-time-manager training gap**: Promote ICs to manager without training → predictable failure + regrettable attrition. Program is investment, not cost.
- **Learning stipend under-utilized**: Offered but unclear process + low awareness → 30% utilization. Streamline approval + promote actively.
- **Compliance training perfunctory + annual-only**: Employees click through; retention of material is zero. Micro-learning + scenario-based + continuous is more effective.
- **Skills-marketplace without skills taxonomy**: Marketplace exists but skills aren't defined → poor matching. Skills taxonomy + regular refresh is foundation.
- **Executive coaching for a favored few**: Inequitable access breeds resentment. Clear criteria for eligibility + consistent rollout.
- **Conference attendance concentrated**: Same senior folks go every year. Equitable access + post-conference share-back expectation.
- **Mentor programs without facilitation**: Assigned pairs, no touchpoint cadence, no check-in. Fades in 60 days.
- **L&D disconnected from performance**: Learning plans don't reflect performance review gaps. Connect reviews → development plans → learning paths.
- **Learning effectiveness measured by completion**: Completion ≠ learning ≠ behavior change. Measure skills progression + role performance + retention correlation.

## Maintain — Keeping It Healthy

### The Rhythm

- **Monthly**: Learning engagement dashboards, compliance-completion tracking, stipend utilization.
- **Quarterly**: Leadership-program progress, skills-gap review, mentor-program check-ins, program NPS surveys.
- **Semi-annually**: Curriculum review, content refresh, program evaluation, learning-stipend benchmarking.
- **Annually**: Budget planning, program strategy, compliance-training renewal, L&D effectiveness report to leadership.
- **Ongoing**: Learning-path updates as roles evolve; conference requests; coaching engagements.

### What to Watch

- **Learning engagement rate**: Active learners / total employees monthly. Target 70%+.
- **Stipend utilization**: % of allocated budget used. Target 75%+. Below signals awareness/process issues.
- **Compliance-completion rate**: 100% on-schedule target. Any miss = legal exposure tracking.
- **Leadership-program completion + impact**: Completion rate, post-program performance/retention, 360 feedback changes.
- **Mentor-program engagement**: Active pair rate (monthly touchpoints). Target 80%+.
- **Skills-growth data**: Skills-taxonomy scores pre/post development investment.
- **Promotion rate correlation**: % of promoted employees who completed development programs.
- **Retention correlation**: Attrition rate difference — learning-program participants vs non-participants.
- **L&D NPS**: "I have opportunities to learn and grow" engagement score. Target 8+/10.

### Exception Handling

- **High-cost learning request**: Tuition reimbursement, executive MBA, fellowship. Decision framework: role relevance + development benefit + retention commitment (e.g., 1–2 year stay post-completion).
- **Compliance training failure**: Escalation path + consequence clear (access suspension, PIP for repeat, termination for severe). Handle firmly but consistently.
- **Manager identified as coaching issue via 360**: Development plan + mandatory first-time-manager (or refresher) program + coaching. Evaluation in 6 months.
- **Knowledge-base degradation**: Quarterly audit, assign owners, refresh cadence. Stale content worse than absent content.
- **Conference-approval inequity**: Data review — who's going, who's requesting, who's rejected. Systemic bias vs real criteria issue.
- **Mentor mismatch**: Either party requests change. Agent rematches based on feedback.
- **Skills-marketplace low utilization**: Skills-taxonomy refresh + communication + manager-engagement to surface opportunities.
- **Executive coaching engagement-issue**: Coach-client fit is critical; offer coach switch after one engagement.

## Scale — Growing It

### Adding Complexity

- **Global L&D delivery**: Country-specific localization (language, cultural context, regulatory), time-zone coordination, local vendors where needed.
- **Technical-ladder certifications**: Internal certifications for technical career growth — data engineering, SRE, ML engineering specialization tracks.
- **Academy-style programs**: Large-scale programs (e.g., Engineering Academy, Sales Academy) with cohort pipelines feeding career growth.
- **University + early-career programs**: Partnerships with universities, formal internship-to-full-time, early-career cohort development.
- **Executive education**: Harvard, Wharton, Stanford, Singularity executive programs. ROI + strategic investment.
- **Coaching scale-out**: Coaching expanding from exec-only to middle-management via scale platforms (BetterUp, Torch, Sounding Board).

### Automation Opportunities

- **Personalized learning paths**: Agent curates continuously based on role + performance + goals + learning pace + industry shifts.
- **Just-in-time learning**: Agent surfaces relevant learning at moments of need (new role, new project, new technology).
- **Skills-analytics**: Agent maps organizational skills inventory vs strategic needs; identifies gaps + L&D investment priorities.
- **Mentor matching optimization**: Agent learns from successful pairings; improves future matches.
- **Manager coaching**: Agent delivers just-in-time manager guidance from review quality analysis, 1:1 note analysis, feedback signals.
- **Leadership-potential identification**: Agent identifies high-potential employees from performance + growth + influence signals; feeds succession planning.

### When You Outgrow This Tier

Move to the **1k+ org** playbook when:

- Dedicated L&D team of 10+ with specialists (instructional designer, program manager, coach).
- Academy-style programs at scale.
- Global delivery with localization + regional ops.
- Internal certifications with rigor (tech ladders, sales cert, leadership cert).
- L&D analytics function with predictive modeling.
- Executive education + coaching programs strategic-talent-management tools.

## By Industry (at this scale)

1. **SaaS / Tech**: Engineering career growth critical for retention. Technical-ladder + tech-conference culture. Internal tech talks. Product + Design development programs.
2. **Professional Services**: Practice-area specialization. Consultant-advancement programs. Industry certifications + continuing education.
3. **Sales-Heavy Orgs**: Multi-week ramp programs. Sales certifications. Leadership development for sales managers.
4. **Healthcare**: Continuing medical education + clinical skill development. Compliance-heavy (HIPAA, licensing). Patient-safety programs.
5. **Manufacturing**: Safety certifications mandatory. Technical/trade certifications. Lean/Six Sigma programs.
6. **Construction / Trades**: Safety + trade certifications + advancement pipelines. Apprenticeship programs where applicable.
7. **Financial Services**: Regulatory continuing education (Series licenses, CPE, compliance certifications). Strong leadership-development investment.
8. **Restaurants / Hospitality**: Operational training + customer service + leadership pipelines for district/GM tracks.
9. **Nonprofit**: Mission-related training + operational skills. Grant-reporting + compliance training.
10. **Creative / Agency**: Craft evolution + tool training + client-management skills. Portfolio-building programs.
