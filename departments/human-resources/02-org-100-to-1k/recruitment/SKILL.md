# Recruitment — 100 to 1,000 People

## What This Process Does

Recruiting at this scale is **a specialized team with a structured pipeline and strategic hiring plan.** A head of recruiting (reporting to CHRO or CEO) leads 2–10 recruiters + sourcers + coordinators. Hiring happens across engineering, sales, marketing, product, ops, finance, HR itself. 50–200 hires per year. Formal hiring plans tied to annual + quarterly planning. Employer-brand program generates inbound + supports employer reputation.

The work: **execute hiring volume with quality, meet strategic role-filling targets, maintain candidate-experience standards, manage hiring-process consistency, and scale without losing bar.** Bad recruiting decisions at this scale cost $50K–$200K per regrettable hire + opportunity cost. Recruiting org itself is $500K–$2M in fully-loaded cost.

## Build — Setting It Up

### With Agents

- **Hiring-plan orchestration**: Agent manages hiring plan with business — headcount by function, level, quarter. Tracks against plan.
- **Sourcing automation**: Agent continuously surfaces candidates across LinkedIn, applicants, referrals, talent communities. Personalized outreach at scale.
- **Intake + pipeline**: Hiring manager submits role → agent drafts JD, interviews, scorecards, comp band → recruiter refines → role launches.
- **Interview orchestration**: Agent books complex multi-day panels across time zones. Handles rescheduling. Provides prep materials to interviewers.
- **Scorecard consistency**: Agent ensures scorecards completed per structure. Aggregates for debriefs.
- **Offer generation**: Agent drafts offers from comp band + role + equity grant. Compliance-checked. Hiring manager reviews.
- **Reference-check scaling**: Agent conducts structured async reference checks at volume.
- **Candidate-experience monitoring**: Agent surveys post-stage, aggregates NPS, flags process issues.
- **Hiring-analytics dashboards**: Pipeline velocity, conversion rates, time-to-hire by role, source quality, offer-accept rates — real-time.

### Key Decisions

1. **Recruiter specialization vs generalization**: Specialized recruiters (engineering vs sales vs GTM) for functional expertise. Generalists for operational efficiency. Hybrid common.
2. **In-house vs agency**: Mostly in-house at this scale. Retained firms for executive. Occasional contingent for specialized/urgent.
3. **Sourcing mix**: Passive outreach (40–60% of hires from outbound sourcing), inbound applications (15–30%), referrals (15–30%), agencies (5–10%).
4. **Interview-loop standardization**: Per role family — engineering (4–6 rounds), sales (4–5), leadership (5–7). Structured per loop.
5. **Hiring committee vs hiring manager**: Hiring committee (3–5 people) for senior/specialist roles; hiring manager decides for IC. Committee structure documented.
6. **Comp-band transparency**: Internal bands published (transparency = competitive advantage). Where legally required, in JDs (NY, CO, CA, etc.).
7. **Employer-brand investment**: Content (blog, engineering blog, podcast), events (meetups, conferences, talks), candidate experience. Material investment pays off in inbound + recruiting efficiency.
8. **DEI strategy**: Sourcing pipelines (diverse candidate pool goals), structured interviews, bias training, representation tracking + accountability.

### Common Mistakes

- **Hiring-plan disconnect from business**: Recruiting team targeting 50 hires while business revises down to 25. Waste + frustration.
- **Pipeline-velocity degradation**: Long loops, slow feedback, slow offers. Top candidates lost to faster competitors.
- **Interview-bar inconsistency**: Manager A hires "good enough"; Manager B holds out for exceptional. Team composition uneven.
- **Over-sourcing, under-converting**: High sourcing volume, low interview conversion. Better-quality sourcing > more volume.
- **Candidate-experience drift at scale**: Slow responses, unclear process, silent rejections. Glassdoor reputation suffers.
- **Employer-brand under-investment**: Recruiting treated as pure execution function without marketing investment. Higher cost per hire, lower quality.
- **DEI as metrics-only**: Representation-goals-focused without sourcing pipeline + interview process changes. Pipeline + process + culture all required.
- **Comp calibration failures**: Offer below band and fail to close; offer above band causing internal equity issues.

## Maintain — Keeping It Healthy

### The Rhythm

- **Weekly**: Pipeline reviews per recruiter with hiring manager. Bottleneck identification + resolution.
- **Daily**: Sourcing outreach volume. Scheduling turnaround. Feedback collection.
- **Monthly**: Hiring-plan vs actual analysis. Team metrics review. Employer-brand activity review.
- **Quarterly**: Hiring-plan refresh. Team retrospective. Employer-brand strategy review.
- **Annually**: Hiring plan for next year. Recruiting team capacity planning. Employer-brand content calendar.

### What to Watch

- **Hiring-plan attainment**: % of planned hires filled on schedule. Track by function.
- **Time-to-hire**: Target 30–45 days IC, 60–90 days senior. Rising = bottleneck investigation.
- **Offer-accept rate**: 80%+ target. Lower = offer competitiveness, experience, or reputation issue.
- **Pass-through rates**: Funnel conversion per stage. Low rates diagnostic.
- **Source-of-hire quality**: 6-month retention + performance by source. Best sources get more investment.
- **Candidate-NPS**: Post-stage + post-decision. Target 9+/10.
- **Recruiter productivity**: Hires per recruiter per quarter. Benchmarked against industry.
- **Pipeline diversity**: Funnel-stage representation for underrepresented groups. Drop-offs investigated.

### Exception Handling

- **Role open >90 days**: Reassessment. JD issue? Comp issue? Bar issue? Market conditions? Hiring freeze?
- **Hiring manager consistently fails to hire**: Coaching, interview-training, possible bar recalibration discussion.
- **Regrettable attrition in 6 months of hire**: Debrief — recruiting process, onboarding, role fit. Adjust if patterns emerge.
- **Counter-offer from current employer**: Expect it at executive levels. Negotiate hard on what matters; don't chase infinitely.
- **Background-check concern**: Consult legal. Ban-the-box compliance. Document decision.
- **Offer-process leak**: Internal details shared externally. Confidentiality training + agreement review.
- **Employer-brand crisis (Glassdoor, news)**: Response strategy with PR + leadership. Don't suppress; address.
- **Hiring-plan cut mid-quarter**: Recruiter capacity reallocated. Pipeline management decision per open role.

## Scale — Growing It

### Adding Complexity

- **International hiring at scale**: Country-specific recruiting partners, comp bands, employment laws, visa sponsorship.
- **University / early-career programs**: Campus recruiting, internship programs, new-grad pipelines.
- **Executive search team**: Dedicated senior-search recruiters + relationship with retained firms.
- **Talent-community + pipeline programs**: Nurture pools of candidates for future roles.
- **M&A recruiting**: Acquired-company integration + retention packages + consolidation.

### Automation Opportunities

- **Intelligent sourcing**: Agent continuously surfaces best-fit passive candidates; personalized outreach; reply-rate optimization.
- **Full-auto scheduling**: Candidate self-serves interview times; agent handles rescheduling; all interviewers calendar-managed.
- **Scorecard + debrief synthesis**: Agent compiles scorecards, identifies consensus/disagreement, drafts debrief agenda.
- **Candidate-experience interventions**: Agent detects disengagement signals, triggers re-engagement actions.
- **Market-intelligence-driven comp**: Agent tracks market comp, flags when offers are below market + recommends adjustments.

### When You Outgrow This Tier

Move to the **1k+ org** playbook when:

- Hiring passes 200/year — recruiting org structure + specialization deepen.
- Global footprint in 10+ countries — distributed recruiting team.
- Campus recruiting major — university relations function.
- Employer-brand function matures — dedicated marketing + events + content team.
- Executive search becomes a recurring function — dedicated executive-search leader.

## By Industry (at this scale)

1. **SaaS / Tech**: Engineering recruiting dominant. Sales recruiting for scaling GTM. Product/design roles competitive.
2. **Professional Services**: Consulting hiring scaled + specialized. Practice-area focus. Campus + experienced hires.
3. **E-commerce**: Operational scale hiring (fulfillment, ops) + corporate. Peak-season surge hiring.
4. **Healthcare**: Licensed-role recruiting (nurses, physicians, clinicians). Credentialing + privileging flow. Shortage-driven market.
5. **Manufacturing**: Skilled-trade apprentice pipelines. Technical recruiting + union-representation considerations.
6. **Retail**: Regional-recruiting structure for locations. Store manager + district manager pipelines.
7. **Financial Services**: Regulatory-cleared hiring processes. Background checks rigorous. Compensation bands structured.
8. **Creative / Agency**: Portfolio-based evaluation. Freelance-to-hire pipelines.
