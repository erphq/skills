# Campaign Management — 100 to 1,000 People

## What This Process Does

Campaign management at this scale is **a multi-channel orchestration engine driving sourced pipeline at scale**. A CMO + 10–30+ marketing team (specialized: demand gen, content, events, marketing ops, brand, web, customer marketing) runs 20–50+ campaigns per quarter across content, paid, events, ABM, partner, customer marketing. Marketo / HubSpot Enterprise / Salesforce Marketing Cloud handles automation; CMS (Webflow, HubSpot CMS, Contentful); attribution (Bizible / Dreamdata / Hightouch + native CRM); demand-side platforms (LinkedIn Ads, Google Ads, Meta, Programmatic); MarTech stack of 30–80 tools.

The work: **deliver sourced-pipeline at quarterly targets, attribute outcomes credibly, optimize spend allocation across channels, build durable brand + audience, and scale without losing rigor.** Marketing is held accountable for sourced-pipeline metric (~30–50% of total pipeline); GTM alignment with sales is critical.

## Build — Setting It Up

### With Agents

- **Campaign-planning + brief workflow**: Agent supports brief creation (target, offer, channel mix, timeline, budget, KPIs); approval; resource allocation.
- **Multi-channel orchestration**: Email + paid + content + event + outbound + ABM coordinated for integrated campaigns.
- **Landing page + asset generation**: Agent drafts landing pages from briefs using high-converting patterns; CMS publishing; UTM capture.
- **Email-sequence creation**: Drip nurture, post-event follow-up, win-back, milestone-based. Personalized by persona + behavior.
- **Paid-campaign optimization**: Daily budget pacing, bid adjustments, audience targeting, creative rotation. Real-time + reactive.
- **ABM orchestration**: Target-account list → coordinated multi-channel touch (paid display + LinkedIn Ads + email + outbound + partner + content + intent-data activation).
- **Webinar + event production**: Promotion + registration + reminders + post-event follow-up + sales handoff.
- **Attribution + analytics**: Multi-touch attribution model (typically W-shaped or U-shaped); first-touch + last-touch + multi-touch; sourced-pipeline + influenced-pipeline tracking.
- **Predictive performance modeling**: Agent forecasts campaign outcomes from historical patterns + current signals.
- **MarTech-integration orchestration**: 30–80 tools sync via integration platform (Workato, Tray, native).

### Key Decisions

1. **Channel mix**: Typical mid-market — content/SEO 25%, paid 25–35%, events 15–20%, outbound 10–15%, partnerships 10%, ABM 10–15%. Evolves per category + ICP.
2. **Budget allocation**: 8–12% of revenue typical for B2B SaaS. Distributed by channel ROI, sourcing capacity.
3. **MQL definition + handoff**: Aligned with sales. SQL-acceptance-rate as quality metric.
4. **Marketing automation platform**:
   - **Marketo (Adobe)**: Enterprise-grade, complex, expensive ($25K+/year base).
   - **HubSpot Enterprise**: Strong mid-market, integrated CRM, simpler.
   - **Salesforce Marketing Cloud**: Salesforce-customer default; complex for non-CRM-marketing flows.
   - **Pardot (Salesforce)**: Mid-market within Salesforce ecosystem.
   - **Eloqua (Oracle)**: Enterprise legacy.
5. **Attribution model**: Single-touch (first or last), W-shaped, U-shaped, time-decay, custom. Multi-touch more accurate; harder to maintain.
6. **ABM strategy**: 1:1 (top accounts), 1:few (industry/persona segments), 1:many (broader programmatic). Allocation per strategic priority.
7. **Content strategy + velocity**: Content team produces 8–25 substantive pieces per month (blog, whitepaper, video, podcast, webinar, customer story).
8. **Customer marketing investment**: Distinct function for retention + expansion + advocacy. 10–25% of total marketing spend.
9. **Brand vs demand spend split**: Typically 70% demand / 30% brand at this stage; varies by stage + category.

### Common Mistakes

- **Vanity-metric celebration**: Impressions + opens + downloads without pipeline impact. Marketing-bubble activity disconnected from business.
- **Attribution disagreement = perpetual conflict**: Sales credits referral; marketing credits campaign that influenced. Multi-touch attribution + joint definition required.
- **MQL volume without quality**: Marketing incentivized on count; floods sales with junk. Adjust to MQL-acceptance-rate + sourced-pipeline.
- **Content production without distribution plan**: Articles published, nobody promotes, 50 views. Distribution = 50% of content work.
- **MarTech tool sprawl**: 80 tools, 60% used, integration debt accumulates.
- **Brand vs demand chronic underinvestment**: All-demand companies build no brand equity; later struggle with awareness. Balance.
- **ABM as just paid display targeted at accounts**: Real ABM = orchestrated multi-channel; one-channel-at-target = demand-gen with target-list.
- **Customer marketing afterthought**: New-business focus eclipses retention + expansion content. Suboptimal.
- **PR / analyst-relations underinvested**: Industry analysts + PR drive credibility + amplification. Worth dedicated function at scale.

## Maintain — Keeping It Healthy

### The Rhythm

- **Daily**: Paid-campaign monitoring + optimization. Web + ABM analytics review.
- **Weekly**: Cross-campaign performance + pipeline-source reporting. Demand-gen team standup.
- **Bi-weekly**: Marketing leadership all-hands. Sales-marketing alignment meeting.
- **Monthly**: Sourced-pipeline + closed-revenue reporting to leadership. Channel-ROI deep-dive. Budget reallocation if needed.
- **Quarterly**: Campaign planning for next quarter. Channel-mix review. ABM-target-account refresh.
- **Annually**: Marketing plan + budget for next year. MarTech stack review. Brand-strategy review.

### What to Watch

- **Sourced-pipeline**: $ pipeline attributed to marketing campaigns. Primary KPI.
- **Sourced-revenue**: $ closed-won attributed. Lagging indicator.
- **Pipeline coverage**: Sourced-pipeline / quarterly quota. Target 1.5–2× from marketing.
- **MQL volume + quality (SQL-acceptance-rate)**: Volume targets + quality 60–80% acceptance.
- **Cost per MQL / SQL / sourced opp**: Trending; channel-specific.
- **Conversion rates**: Visitor → lead → MQL → SQL → opp → CW. Each stage tracked.
- **Channel ROI**: $ pipeline + $ revenue per $ marketing spend by channel.
- **ABM target-account engagement**: % of named accounts engaged + advancing.
- **Content performance**: Traffic, leads, pipeline per content piece. Winners get amplified.
- **Brand metrics**: Aided + unaided awareness, share of voice, sentiment.
- **Retention / NRR contribution**: Customer-marketing impact on renewal + expansion.

### Exception Handling

- **Campaign underperforms by 50%**: Mid-campaign intervention. Adjust creative, targeting, budget, offer. Kill if unsalvageable.
- **High-volume lead spike**: Quality validation; rate-limit if needed; sales capacity check.
- **PR crisis or product issue**: Pause campaigns. Messaging review + adjust.
- **Major outage on marketing automation**: Manual workarounds; communicate; vendor escalation.
- **Compliance issue (CAN-SPAM, GDPR)**: Legal review. Content adjustments. Audit existing campaigns.
- **Budget cut mid-quarter**: Reassess priorities — protect highest-ROI; cut experimental + low-performers.
- **Sales-marketing escalation**: Joint-leadership review. Clarify SLAs + definitions + handoffs.
- **Major-deal influence vs sourced-credit dispute**: Multi-touch attribution shows shared credit.
- **PR / analyst-relations critical event**: Coordinated response across PR + executive + product.

## Scale — Growing It

### Adding Complexity

- **Multi-product marketing**: Each product line with distinct positioning, audience, channel mix; cross-product campaigns coordinated.
- **International marketing**: Region-specific channels, localized content, country-specific compliance.
- **Multi-segment GTM**: SMB inbound vs Enterprise ABM vs Mid-Market hybrid; distinct strategies + tools.
- **Customer-marketing program scale**: Customer advisory board, advocacy program, customer-led growth.
- **Brand + thought-leadership investment**: Editorial team, executive thought-leadership, conference speaking.
- **Partner marketing**: MDF (Market Development Funds), partner co-marketing, joint campaigns.

### Automation Opportunities

- **AI-driven campaign optimization**: Real-time bid + audience + creative optimization beyond rule-based.
- **Predictive campaign-performance**: Agent forecasts MQL + pipeline based on campaign characteristics + historical patterns.
- **Personalization at scale**: Multi-variant personalized content + email per persona × industry × stage.
- **Content-performance analytics**: Predict winning content patterns; suggest topics + formats.
- **Attribution refinement AI**: Continuously improve attribution model from closed-won outcomes.
- **ABM-orchestration intelligence**: Agent identifies high-engagement target accounts + recommends next plays.

### When You Outgrow This Tier

Move to the **1k+ org** playbook when:

- Marketing team passes 50+; deeper specialization (PR, AR, executive comms, brand) needed.
- Multi-product portfolio with distinct GTM motions per product.
- Global operations with regional marketing leadership.
- Brand + creative function distinct from demand gen.
- MarTech stack passes 100 tools; dedicated MarTech ops team.

## By Industry (at this scale)

1. **SaaS / Subscription**: Content + paid + events + outbound. ABM for enterprise. Product-led-growth signals integrated.
2. **Professional Services**: Thought-leadership + industry events + ABM. Reputation marketing dominates.
3. **E-commerce (B2B)**: Trade shows + distributor enablement + PLA (Product Listing Ads).
4. **Manufacturing**: Technical content + conferences + distributor channel. Long sales cycles + nurture.
5. **Healthcare (B2B)**: Industry events + credentialed introductions + regulatory-bound messaging.
6. **Financial Services (B2B)**: COI networks + industry conferences + regulated content.
7. **Construction**: Trade publications + project pipeline data + referral networks.
8. **Marketing / Agency**: Portfolio + case studies + thought leadership + RFP responses.
9. **Education / Nonprofit (B2B)**: Institution + funding-source segmented + academic-calendar driven.
10. **Real Estate / Property**: Market-data driven + relationship-based.
